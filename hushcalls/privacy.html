Hush Calls Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Hush Calls collects no data. Everything you set up stays on your iPhone.

Hush Calls is an iPhone app that blocks incoming calls from number prefixes you choose. It is made by Aleksandrs Kalinins.

What the app collects

Nothing. Hush Calls does not collect, upload, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

The prefixes you add, their labels, your country settings and the list of numbers they cover are saved only on your iPhone. The app hands that list to iOS so it can block matching calls.

Your calls and contacts

Hush Calls never sees your calls, call history or contacts. iOS compares incoming calls with the list itself, and the app is not told when a call arrives or is blocked.

Network

The only time the app goes online is when you open its About screen: it asks the App Store whether a newer version of Hush Calls exists. That request contains no personal data, and nothing you set up in the app is ever sent anywhere.

Deleting your data

Delete a prefix in the app to remove it, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
