Hush Calls Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Hush Calls collects no data. What you set up stays on your iPhone and, if you leave iCloud sync on, in your own iCloud account.

Hush Calls is an iPhone app that blocks incoming calls from number prefixes you choose. It is made by Aleksandrs Kalinins.

What the app collects

Nothing. Hush Calls does not collect, upload, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

The prefixes you add, their labels, your country settings and the list of numbers they cover are saved on your iPhone. The app hands that list to iOS so it can block matching calls.

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, Hush Calls keeps a copy of your prefixes, their labels and your country settings in your own iCloud account, so they come back if you reinstall the app and stay the same on your other devices. Apple stores this copy under its own privacy policy. It is private to your Apple Account, and the developer of Hush Calls cannot see it. You can turn this off in the app's Settings, which removes the copy from iCloud.

Your calls and contacts

Hush Calls never sees your calls, call history or contacts. iOS compares incoming calls with the list itself, and the app is not told when a call arrives or is blocked.

Network

The only time the app goes online is when you open its About screen: it asks the App Store whether a newer version of Hush Calls exists. That request contains no personal data. Apart from iCloud sync, nothing you set up in the app is sent anywhere.

Deleting your data

Delete a prefix in the app to remove it, everywhere it syncs. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone; to also remove the iCloud copy, turn off Sync with iCloud in the app's Settings before deleting it.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
